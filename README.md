# The Train of Seven Doors · Le Train des sept portes · El tren de las siete puertas

A short fairy-tale adventure in seven carriages. You wake in a velvet seat under a
window full of stars, with a note in your own handwriting: *seven doors between here
and the engine — reach it, then decide what the fire becomes.*

**Play**: open `index.html` in any browser (self-contained, no dependencies, no
network). Also published on LOB7.

- **Runs**: ~10–15 minutes, two endings.
- **Languages**: English (default), Français, Español — switch any time from the header.
- **Controls**: mouse/touch (buttons), or `Enter` to confirm, `1–4` action shortcuts.

## How to play

- Cross **seven carriages** to reach the midnight locomotive.
- At forks, choose your way: a **fight**, an **encounter**, or **supplies**.
- Fights are **turn-based**. Every foe shows its **intention** before you act —
  guard the big telegraphed blows, strike the openings.
- **Strike** is free. **Guard** raises a shield (7) and restores 2 energy.
  **Lantern Flash** costs 4 energy, hits for 10 and ignores enemy guards.
- Some doors grant **items** or a choice of **two upgrades**. Items genuinely
  change your options (a punched ticket softens inspectors, a lantern feeds your
  energy, a copper star empowers every third strike…).
- Lose a fight and the train carries you back — retry the wagon at 70% health,
  keeping everything you gathered.
- Two endings wait at the fire. **No random drops**: every reward is a deliberate
  choice, and the game is winnable without relying on any particular item.

## The bestiary

Three regular foes and a final adversary in two phases: **dust wisps** (fast, weak,
energy-thieves), the **ravenous trunk** (telegraphed chomp), the **faceless
conductors** (heavy stamps, cap-blocked), and the **Midnight Engineer** — the
Stoker, then the drive itself.

## Development

- `index.html` is the whole game: markup, CSS, two scripts — `train-core` (pure
  rules engine, no DOM: state machine, combat math, i18n dictionaries, stage
  graph) and `train-ui` (rendering, SVG art, WebAudio synth, save/continue).
- Randomness is a seeded mulberry32; saves restore it exactly.
- QA hook: `window.__train` (state, view, render, setLang) — used by the browser
  verification sessions and available for tinkering.
- Tests live outside this repository (workshop `games/train-sept-portes-dev/`):
  16 Node tests run against the real `index.html` core (combat math, intents,
  items, upgrades, both endings, winnability without items across seeds,
  i18n parity EN/FR/ES, determinism, save/load round-trip).
